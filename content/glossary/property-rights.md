# Property Rights

[[glossary/institution|Institutional]] [[glossary/structure|structures]] defining who can use, modify, transfer, and exclude others from resources — the foundation of [[glossary/market|markets]] and economic coordination.

Property rights are bundles of entitlements: use rights, transfer rights, exclusion rights, rights to returns. Different configurations (private, common, public, open-access) create different incentive [[glossary/structure|structures]] and outcomes.

## Systems Connection

Property rights define [[glossary/boundary|boundaries]] around resources — who is inside (entitled) and outside (excluded). They enable [[glossary/market|market]] [[glossary/flow|flows]] by making exchange possible: you can only trade what you own. Clear property rights create [[glossary/feedback-loop|feedback]] between effort and reward, incentivizing investment and stewardship.

## Key Dimensions

- **Clarity** — how well-defined are boundaries?
- **Enforceability** — can violations be detected and punished?
- **Transferability** — can rights be exchanged?
