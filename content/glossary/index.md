# Glossary

Foundational terms for systems thinking — formal definitions grounded in Mobus & Kalton and Bunge, with links to deeper explorations.

## The Basics

- [[glossary/system|System]] — the fundamental unit of analysis
- [[glossary/boundary|Boundary]] — what separates inside from outside
- [[glossary/environment|Environment]] — everything outside the boundary

## The Parts

- [[glossary/component|Component]] — distinct parts performing functions
- [[glossary/subsystem|Subsystem]] — components that are themselves systems
- [[glossary/interface|Interface]] — where exchanges happen

## The Dynamics

- [[glossary/flow|Flow]] — movement of matter, energy, messages
- [[glossary/state|State]] — system configuration at a moment
- [[glossary/feedback-loop|Feedback Loop]] — circular causality
- [[glossary/adaptation|Adaptation]] — self-modification in response to change

## The Patterns

- [[glossary/structure|Structure]] — how components are organized
- [[glossary/function|Function]] — what systems do
- [[glossary/emergence|Emergence]] — wholes with properties parts lack
