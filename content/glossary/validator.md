# Validator

A [[glossary/node|node]] that participates in [[glossary/consensus-mechanism|consensus]] — proposing or attesting to blocks in exchange for rewards.

In Proof of Stake systems, validators stake collateral ([[glossary/tokenomics|tokens]]) as security deposit. Honest behavior earns rewards; misbehavior triggers slashing (loss of stake). In Proof of Work, the equivalent role is "miner" — competing through computational work.

## Systems Connection

Validators are the active [[glossary/component|components]] in blockchain [[glossary/governance|governance]]. They perform the [[glossary/function|function]] of state transition: deciding which transactions to include and in what order. The [[glossary/mechanism-design|mechanism design]] of validator incentives determines whether the system achieves [[glossary/decentralization|decentralization]] or concentrates power.

## Key Dynamics

- **Block production** — validators propose new blocks
- **Attestation** — validators vote on block validity
- **Reward/slashing** — [[glossary/feedback-loop|feedback]] aligning incentives
