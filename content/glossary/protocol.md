# Protocol

The complete rule set defining a cryptoeconomic [[glossary/system|system]] — specifying valid [[glossary/state|states]], allowed transitions, and [[glossary/component|component]] interactions.

A protocol establishes the [[glossary/boundary|boundary]] of the system: what transactions are valid, how consensus is reached, what constitutes correct behavior. Examples include Bitcoin (UTXO model, Nakamoto consensus) and Ethereum (account model, EVM execution).

## Systems Connection

The protocol defines the [[glossary/system|system's]] [[glossary/boundary|boundary]] and [[glossary/structure|structure]] — the rules that all [[glossary/component|components]] must follow. Protocol upgrades are a form of [[glossary/adaptation|adaptation]]: the system modifies its own rules to respond to changing conditions or discovered vulnerabilities.
