# Mechanism Design

Engineering incentive [[glossary/structure|structures]] to achieve desired [[glossary/emergence|emergent]] outcomes — "reverse game theory" where you design the rules rather than analyze them.

In cryptoeconomics, mechanism design creates protocols where rational actors pursuing self-interest produce beneficial collective outcomes. Examples include auction mechanisms, staking/slashing rules, and governance voting systems.

## Systems Connection

Mechanism design is [[glossary/structure|structural]] engineering for social [[glossary/system|systems]]. The designer cannot control individual behavior but can shape the [[glossary/feedback-loop|feedback loops]] that govern it. A well-designed mechanism makes desirable behavior individually rational — aligning [[glossary/component|component]] incentives with [[glossary/system|system]] goals.
