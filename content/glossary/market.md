# Market

A [[glossary/system|system]] for coordinating exchange through prices — where buyers and sellers interact to allocate resources without central direction.

Markets are the paradigmatic example of [[glossary/spontaneous-order|spontaneous order]]: no one designs prices, yet they emerge to coordinate millions of decisions. Price signals transmit distributed information about scarcity and preferences.

## Systems Connection

A market is a [[glossary/system|system]] whose [[glossary/component|components]] (buyers and sellers) interact through [[glossary/interface|interfaces]] (transactions) with [[glossary/flow|flows]] of goods and money. Prices encode [[glossary/state|state]] information. [[glossary/feedback-loop|Feedback loops]] operate through supply and demand: high prices attract supply and reduce demand until equilibrium emerges.

## Key Properties

- **Price discovery** — emergent information aggregation
- **Allocation** — resources flow to highest-valued uses
- **Incentive alignment** — profit motive coordinates behavior
