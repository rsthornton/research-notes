# Collective Action

Coordination problems where individual rationality leads to collectively suboptimal outcomes — the gap between what's best for each and what's best for all.

Classic examples include the prisoner's dilemma, tragedy of the commons, and free-rider problems. Solutions involve changing incentives, building trust, establishing institutions, or enabling communication and commitment.

## Systems Connection

Collective action problems reveal how [[glossary/component|component]]-level optimization doesn't guarantee [[glossary/system|system]]-level welfare. Each agent responds rationally to local [[glossary/feedback-loop|feedback]], yet the [[glossary/emergence|emergent]] outcome is suboptimal. Solving collective action requires restructuring [[glossary/feedback-loop|feedback loops]] — aligning individual and collective interests through institutional design.
