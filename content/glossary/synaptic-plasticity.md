# Synaptic Plasticity

[[glossary/adaptation|Adaptive]] modification of synaptic strengths based on activity patterns — how neural systems learn through experience.

Key mechanisms include spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP), where synapses strengthen when presynaptic spikes precede postsynaptic spikes and weaken otherwise. Homeostatic plasticity maintains overall activity levels. These local learning rules produce [[glossary/emergence|emergent]] system-level learning.

## Systems Connection

Synaptic plasticity is [[glossary/adaptation|adaptation]] at the [[glossary/component|component]] level. Each synapse adjusts its weight (part of the system [[glossary/state|state]]) based on local information only. Yet these distributed adjustments produce coherent learning at the [[glossary/system|system]] level — an example of [[glossary/emergence|emergence]] from simple local rules.
